📜 Royal Decree & Building Regulations

Win Conditions

  • Every Cell Decided: Every cell must be either a House, a Wall, or marked as Property.
  • Correct Property Size: Each house's connected property must equal the number on the house (including the house cell itself).
  • Connected Properties: Property cells must connect horizontally or vertically (not diagonally).
  • One House per Property: No two houses may belong to the same connected property area.
  • Separated Properties: Different properties must be separated by walls (diagonal corner contact is allowed).
  • Connected Wall System: All wall cells must form a single continuous orthogonal network.
  • No 2×2 Wall Blocks: Walls must never completely fill any 2×2 area.

Controls & Legend

  • Left Click / Drag: Place or remove a stone Wall. Dragging applies the initial action across cells.
  • Right Click / Drag: Mark or unmark a cell as Property (wall-free land).
  • Houses: Fixed on the board and cannot be modified.
  • Red Highlight: Indicates an invalid state (property enclosed and too small, marked property too large, or a 2×2 wall block).
